You have made it to the final lesson of **Module 1: Foundations**.
Let's review the tools we've built from scratch:

1. **Perceptrons and Layers**: We stacked neurons into matrices to build a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP).
2. **Forward Propagation**: We learned how data flows through the network via matrix multiplication and non-linear activations like ReLU.
3. **Loss Functions**: We defined mathematical penalties (like Cross-Entropy) to measure how wrong our predictions are.
4. **Backpropagation**: We used the Chain Rule of calculus to calculate exactly how much each weight contributed to the error.
5. **Gradient Descent & Optimizers**: We updated the weights using Adam to roll down the loss surface as fast as possible.
6. **Regularization**: We added L2 penalties and Dropout to ensure our network generalized instead of just memorizing the data.

### The Capstone Project

Now, it is time to put all of these concepts together to solve a problem that a simple linear model cannot solve: separating a dataset of inner circles from an outer ring.

## The Interactive Playground

Below is a live, completely functional Neural Network running directly in your browser. 
The task is to classify the points on the 2D plane: **Blue** points are class 1, **Orange** points are class 0.

By default, it has 2 hidden layers with 6 neurons each. 
Press **Play** to start the training loop. You will watch the background (the **decision boundary**) warp and fold as the network learns to carve out a circular region in the center of the space to perfectly separate the blue points from the orange points.

Try resetting it, dropping the neurons down to 2, and seeing if it can still solve the problem! (Spoiler: it can't, it doesn't have enough capacity!)
