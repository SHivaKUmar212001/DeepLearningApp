GANs are incredibly powerful, but they have a major flaw: Mode Collapse. 

Because the Generator is constantly trying to trick the Discriminator, it often figures out that drawing *one single perfect cat* tricks the Discriminator 100% of the time. So, it stops trying to draw anything else. It forgets how to draw dogs, cars, or even other types of cats.

To solve this, researchers completely rethought how we generate images, inspired by non-equilibrium thermodynamics. This led to the creation of **Diffusion Models**.

## The Core Concept

Imagine a drop of ink falling into a glass of water. At first, you can clearly see the shape of the ink drop. But over time, the ink diffuses until the water is just a uniform color. The original information (the shape of the drop) has been completely destroyed by entropy.

What if we could train a neural network to run time *backwards*? What if it could look at a uniform glass of water and mathematically un-diffuse it back into a perfect drop of ink?

## The Forward Process (Adding Noise)

To train a network to reverse diffusion, we first have to mathematically define diffusion. We call this the **Forward Process**.

We start with a clean image from our dataset. Over $T = 1000$ tiny time steps, we iteratively add a tiny bit of Gaussian noise to the image. 

This is a **Markov Chain**, meaning the image at step $t=10$ is calculated *only* by taking the image at $t=9$ and adding a little more noise.

By $t=1000$, the image is completely unrecognizable. It is pure, isotropic Gaussian noise. The "Signal" has been completely destroyed by the "Noise".

There is no neural network involved in the Forward Process. It is purely a fixed mathematical equation.

But now that we know exactly *how* the noise was added at every single step, we can train a neural network to predict that noise and remove it!
