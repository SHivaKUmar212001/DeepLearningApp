The original Transformer was built for translation, so it needed both an Encoder and a Decoder. 

But in 2018, researchers at OpenAI asked a question: What if we just throw away the Encoder? What if we just give the Decoder a block of text, and ask it to predict the very next word?

They called this **Generative Pre-trained Transformer**, or **GPT**.

## Auto-Regressive Generation

A Decoder-only model has one single job: Look at the current sequence of words (the **Context Window**), calculate the probabilities for what the next word should be, and sample a word from that distribution.

But how does it write an entire essay? It uses an **Auto-Regressive** loop!

1. Read the Context Window.
2. Predict the next word.
3. **Append** that new word to the end of the Context Window.
4. Go back to Step 1.

## The Scaling Laws

Because GPT models are so incredibly simple (just predict the next word), they are incredibly easy to train on massive datasets. You don't need highly structured, human-translated English-to-French datasets. You can just scrape the entire internet!

OpenAI discovered the "Scaling Laws": As you increase the size of the neural network (parameters) and the size of the dataset (internet text), the model's ability to generate coherent, intelligent text increases predictably. 

This simple Decoder-only architecture, scaled up to massive proportions, is exactly what powers **ChatGPT** today!
